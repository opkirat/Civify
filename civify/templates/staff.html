How this works

Goes live automatically when the quote is found in the report, every number matches, an AI check finds the quote fully supports the sentence, and a second AI check, reading the whole page and told to hold when in doubt, agrees. Sentences about taking part or deadlines never go live this way.

Needs you: about taking part or a deadline, or either AI check raised something. The reason is on each card, often with suggested wording you can approve in one click.

Source check failed: the quote wasn't found or a number doesn't match. Edit it so it passes, or reject it.

Every change makes a new numbered version of the resident page, listed in the history, so you can always see what residents saw and when.
